## 🎛️ A Different Way to Make Music

**Ableton Live 12** is a digital audio workstation designed for music creation, recording, sound design, arrangement and live performance.

Its workflow combines traditional timeline editing with a flexible Session View for experimentation and performance.

---

# 🟦 SESSION VIEW

## Build Without Limits

Launch clips and scenes to experiment with musical ideas without committing to a traditional timeline.

Create:

* Beats
* Loops
* Melodies
* Basslines
* Vocal ideas
* Live arrangements

Experiment first. Arrange later.

---

# 🟨 ARRANGEMENT VIEW

## Turn Ideas Into Tracks

Move from experimentation to a traditional linear timeline.

Organize:

```text id="abflow"
IDEAS
  ↓
CLIPS
  ↓
ARRANGEMENT
  ↓
EDIT
  ↓
MIX
  ↓
MASTER
```

Create complete songs while keeping the flexibility of clip-based production.

---

# 🎹 MIDI

## Compose With Precision

Create and edit MIDI performances using detailed note controls.

Work with:

* Melodies
* Chords
* Basslines
* Drums
* Automation
* Instrument performances

Use MIDI editing tools to shape timing, velocity and musical expression.

---

# 🎚️ MIXING

## Shape Every Track

Build mixes with Live's audio effects and routing capabilities.

Use tools for:

* EQ
* Compression
* Reverb
* Delay
* Saturation
* Dynamics
* Filtering
* Spatial effects

Create effect chains and automate parameters throughout a track.

---

# 🔊 SOUND DESIGN

## Build Your Own Sound

Live includes instruments, effects and devices for creating and processing sounds.

Explore:

* Synthesizers
* Samplers
* Drum instruments
* Audio effects
* MIDI effects
* Modulation
* Granular workflows

Combine devices to create custom instruments and processing chains.

---

# 🥁 DRUM PRODUCTION

Build rhythms using dedicated drum workflows.

Create:

* Electronic beats
* Hip-hop drums
* House grooves
* Techno patterns
* Experimental percussion
* Sample-based rhythms

Layer samples and effects to develop your own sound.

---

# 🎤 RECORDING

## Capture Ideas Immediately

Record vocals, instruments and external audio sources using supported hardware.

Use recordings for:

* Vocals
* Guitar
* Piano
* Live instruments
* Field recordings
* Sampling

---

# 🔄 AUDIO WARPING

## Change Time Without Losing The Idea

Warping allows audio clips to be manipulated within the project tempo.

Use it to:

* Synchronize recordings
* Experiment with tempo
* Create loops
* Rearrange samples
* Transform performances

---

# 🎹 PUSH INTEGRATION

Ableton Live is designed to work with Ableton's Push hardware ecosystem.

Use compatible hardware workflows for hands-on control of:

* Instruments
* Clips
* Drums
* Melodies
* Effects
* Mixing

---
